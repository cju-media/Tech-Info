Wake now my senses, and hear the Earth call,
Feel the deep power of being in all,
Keep with the web of creation your vow,
Giving, receiving, as love shows us how.

Wake now my reason, reach out for the new,
Join with each trav’ller who quests for the true,
Honor the beauty and wisdom of time,
Transcend our limits and touch the sublime.

Wake now my vision, the dream we hold dear,
Brighten our pathway with radiance here,
Journey together with all who will share,
Love for the planet transformed by our care.
