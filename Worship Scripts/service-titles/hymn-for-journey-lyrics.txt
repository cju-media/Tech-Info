God the Creator gave in abundance
All of the wonder we now behold;
Colors of glory, everyday beauty,
Echo the story of God’s deep love.

Bold as the sunlight, tender as nightfall,
Love shaped the heav’n’s height; love filled the seas.
Love formed the flowers, called for the living;
Marked out the hours of time to be.

Come, see the fullness – Life we’ve been given;
Beauty and promise, mystr’y and hope.
Laughter and weeping, vision and purpose;
Friends for the keeping, strength for the road.

Open your hearts wide, teach us your wisdom
Lest our days pass by empty and small.
May we be witness with your creation –
To your great kindness given to all.
