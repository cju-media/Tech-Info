For all the saints, who from their labors rest,
Who in the world their faith in God confessed,
Your name, oh Jesus, be forever blessed:
Alleluia, Alleluia!

You were the stranger in the dark of night
With whom they strove to find their one true light,
To whom you ever give God’s blessing ever bright:
Alleluia, Alleluia!

There are the ones who gave us Love Divine,
Always in service did their wills incline,
Forgetting self, they did with glory shine:
Alleluia, Alleluia!

And there will dawn a yet more glorious day
The saints with laughter sing and dance and play,
The Christ of all rejoices in the way:
Alleluia, Alleluia!

With earth restored, with this our fragile star
In gladness home from pilgrimage afar
We find in God a joy that none can mar:
Alleluia, Alleluia!
